VERDICT: IS THIS GOOD ENOUGH TO DRIVE A TOPP-RA FEASIBILITY CHECK?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NO -- not yet. Velocity is usable on 3 of 6 joints, and only as a floor.
Acceleration is not usable at all. Torque has no empirical route whatsoever.
TOPP-RA needs a velocity AND an acceleration bound per joint, and it is most
sensitive to the acceleration bound -- the one that swings by a factor of five
depending on an arbitrary filter width. Running it now would produce a
confidently-parameterised time-scaling built on a number that is mostly savgol
bandwidth: the same failure mode as S_torque, one layer up.

UNBLOCK: a single-joint ramp test per joint (drive to saturation, log at
>= 200 Hz). That fixes velocity, acceleration, and the qdot_max calibration
Research_notes.txt already flags -- in one session.
